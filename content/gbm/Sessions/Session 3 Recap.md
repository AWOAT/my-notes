## Mission 

The crew went to Cropnik to hunt the Schistatylus, and Adeline tagged along. They perceive her a bit; she’s certainly an alien, and she’s hiding her true intentions for tagging along.

They approached the Schista and were attacked by the Tylus. They mostly got out of the way under Reverb’s leadership.

Claire tried to close the distance to Schista but got body slammed by Tylus against the wall, and she was forced to transcend. Iris used her shields to pull Schista a bit closer. Meanwhile, Reverb and Adeline went for the head. They pierced its hide, and the blood made it easier to track.

Claire goes for Schista but is blindsided by a SECOND Tylus head. It bites off her shoulder. Reverb uses her powers to emerge from the open wound and protect her. She resonates with the Tylus skull and severely damages it, and Iris finishes it off.

Adeline had taken care of the other head, so they slay Schista with no issue. Claire scoops up the fallen whale piece, and Adeline opens the fancy black envelope that was left behind, accepting the invitation to the duel with Kleos. This makes Claire quite mad, and she really wants to take her place in the duel. But they harvest the whale pieces and go on their way.
