## Mission 

The crew went to the [[Bulwark]] to take an elevator up to the [[Harpoon]]’s mansion. They overheard the password and got in, no problem.

Inside, they spoke with a slimy old general, Brother Clarence. They rebranded themselves as Mariah, Fortulazo, and… Mercedes. Hmm.

They made their way to the ballroom. Adaigo provided vocals for the music, allowing the others to operate a bit more at ease. Trying to eavesdrop, Mercedes wandered into the middle of the ballroom where she was asked to dance by Dmitri. Luckily she was saved from embarrassment by Elinalaise, who swiftly took her place. 

Rather unfortunately, Elinalaise quickly caught the eye of [[Hera]], who happened to be infiltrating as well. They had an outwardly polite but rather tense conversation. Hera warned her that something more dangerous was present.

To stay occupied, Mercedes started wandering the hallways. She poked her head into a room that was… occupied. She ducked out quickly in shame, only to be approached by a white-haired lady, asking her what she was doing. She did manage to slip by and reunite with the crew.

Elinalaise noticed that the lady looked awfully like [[Lady Aricia|Kana]], so they tailed her. It led them to a small chapel room. They sat down and waited for a ceremony of some sort. Eventually, the white-haired lady Kit Heartbreak began her sermon, worshiping the whale as a god and praying for its revival. At the end, she asked for volunteers: Brother Lawrence was selected at random, and Kit led him into a backroom while the congregation dispersed.

The crew followed after them: they had to deal with this quickly before they got cornered on this stairway. They saw Kit sharpening an ornate sword, emanating more and more magical energy. Lawrence was seated on a stone chair with something resembling runes drawn on his chest.

Claire charged Kit, knocking the sword away while simultaneously placing a blade on her throat. Kit explained the purpose of the ritual: when someone is decapitated by this blade, their headless body reanimates seven days later. Then it begins attacking aliens (in some rare cases, humans).

Claire let both of them live; they slipped by the oncoming crowd and got out without much trouble. Adaigo held onto the sword as they went their separate ways, job complete.
