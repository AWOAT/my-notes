
## [[Claire Grimoire]]

Song: [Texas Chainsaw Dilemma](https://open.spotify.com/track/7xbkHJ3Sb293jac5RicDn1?si=8uQ17MlXRDGEcPrJzN1Hdw)

You dream of the whale.

You walk through the halls of your prison as the businessman speaks to you. You pass by many locked cells, looking at the prisoners. Most of them are strangers, but you do recognize a few, like [[Soriten]] and [[Nine of Hearts]]. They do not notice you. A few of the cells are open and empty. Presumably they held magical girls once, but it seems they made similar bargains of their own.

“Isn’t it nice to have a little more power?” the whale asks. “Even once you were out of those chains, a single jail cell still isn’t much room to work with. Perhaps it is time to start tearing down the prison walls with your own bare hands. Then again… maybe you could use a tool or two.” It gestures towards a girl you know, asleep in her cell. “Shall we take a look? Off the record, of course.” 

Suddenly, you are looking into an elevator with two people inside. You recognize the elevator: it leads up to the [[Harpoon]]’s mansion. You recognize the people: it is Walt ([[Iris of the Moon|Iris]]’s traitor) and [[Sinia Starr|Sinia]] (in her mundane form). Sinia takes Walt’s arm as they step out of the elevator, and Walt leads her into the party. He introduces his date to all of the Harpoon members he is friendly with, and Sinia very quickly loses track of the names of all these strangers. They dance on the ballroom floor, holding each other closer as the night goes on. 

After what seems like hours, the party comes to a close. Walt did not bring her to the sermon tonight; it seemed better to play it slow. After all, she will surely come again. He walks Sinia home, and as he kisses her hand in farewell, you wake up.

## [[Iris of the Moon]]

Song: [Ego Assembly](https://open.spotify.com/track/7um9qhuog1cQCWwjPm3lHl?si=AiPWVgSnREOiDmFD8rePWQ)

You dream that you are a woman from long ago, in a city you’ve never seen.  
  
You are sneaking through a high-tech shipping yard, weaving between massive cargo containers, hiding in the shadows from the sentries. You ask again for the container label, and in your earpiece, Marshall answers. You grin. It’s right in front of you. You gently crack open the door and go inside, switching on your flashlight.  
  
There are many crates in here, most of them containing junk. But after about fifteen minutes, you pry open a lid and find what you were looking for. Surrounded by packing peanuts, there is a small ivory idol resembling a turtle. It’s real! This is it! Finally… The ritual can be conducted now. You have—  
  
Your fiancé comes back in on the earpiece. Time to go. Quickly. As you hear the beat of a helicopter above, you carefully wrap the turtle and tuck it under your arm like a football. With your heart still racing from the discovery, you steady your breathing—and then you run. As fast as you can. The helicopter above you blares a voice from its loudspeaker. Something  about preparing to open fire. You aren’t listening.  
  
You finally reach the open container where Marshall is waiting with his motorcycle. He revs the engine, slams the door open, and races into the winding streets of the city before the helicopter can even react. You laugh, exhilarated, as together you fade into the neon haze. You kiss the back of his helmet, and you wake up.

## [[Reverb]]

Song: [The Light of an Egress](https://open.spotify.com/track/4Y4Wtw2oKyypYncIbXPXzt?si=c6e35b1aca1f4fde)

You dream that you are walking through the streets of [[Alpha Crest]]. 

As always, it strikes you as out of place in Scourgespire. Strangers nod hello. The night is neon. The air is clean. 

And this really is where the public has the best chance of accessing magic. The tech companies here are not all major conglomerates or [[Bulwark]] puppets. Some of them are starting to create their own versions of technology that WML has been keeping secret, and they are releasing it to the public. Of course, nothing really compares to the magic that magical girls wield. 

“Doesn’t that make you sad, Miss Weaver?”

The voice comes from two people walking ahead of you. You hadn’t noticed them before, but it is the magical girl [[Naroma, the South Star|Naroma]] speaking to their mentor [[Hera]], in their mundane forms. They do not see you; it is your dream, after all. As they continue walking, Hera laughs.

“Please, do not call me Miss Weaver. We’re close enough in age that I would be obligated to call you Miss Mahlon, and I really don’t want to do that, Lilliana.”

“But you’re my mentor. It feels proper.”

“Not right now, I’m not. Only when transcended. Just call me Corrine. Anyways, what were you saying?”

“Umm… oh, right. It makes me sad that we can’t really help people directly with our magic, since it would put too much of a target on our backs. So companies in Alpha Crest have to do a ton of extra work to get to the same level.”

“It is quite unfortunate. But that just makes your work all the more important, does it not?”

“Hmm… yeah. I guess you’re right.”

“Well, I usually am.”

The two smile at each other for a moment before Hera coughs and breaks eye contact. You follow them for another minute before they stop in front of the [[Codex Corps]] building. Lilliana goes inside for their shift, Corrine waves goodbye, and you wake up. 


## Together

A new scandal has come to light, again from the same newspaper, the Slice of Life. This time, it involves Mayor Scott Heffernan, the mayor of [[Block 28]]. It appears he has been covering up tax fraud that has been committed by multiple companies that operate in Block 28. He has stated that the companies' success is vital to the wellbeing of the area, but the article tears into him for being another common crook.

Now, Iris, you've met Scott multiple times. He has frequently organized food drives and other events with the fire department. Claire and Reverb, you two have maybe met him once, but you've certainly overhead people talk about him (maybe in the Slanted Table or Boba Joe's). Quite honestly, people like him. He really has done some good for Block 28. Once this article came out, the area has been a little quieter, a little sadder. 

And the three of you are a little worried about his wellbeing. It's an awfully similar article to the one about Vance Thomas, and he was found dead a few days later, with signs of a magical girl at the scene. You probably don't think Mayor Heffernan should die, but would you go out of your way to save him?

Finally, all three of you had especially interesting dreams, stuff that one of the others might like to know. Feel free to share as much or as little information about them as you would like.
