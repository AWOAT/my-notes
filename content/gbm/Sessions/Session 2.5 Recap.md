
## [[Claire Grimoire]]

Song: [Lost Library](https://open.spotify.com/track/33i4H7iDxIes1d8Nd0S3QF?si=3cff4c20183e4e94)

You dream of a calm ocean. You are standing at the bow of a large boat, calmly looking into the distance. The soft hues of the setting sun seep into the water, refracting over and over again within the waves, transforming the sea into a pastel kaleidoscope. You breathe in; the salty air soothes you.

There are other people on this ship. The captain and crew, of course, but patrons too. Sleazy men, modern women, old money, new money… you know the scene well. But for the first time, they are strangers to you, and you are a stranger to them. You are not alone, but you are certainly on your own.

The sea breeze is so much saltier when you are on your own.

## [[Iris of the Moon]]

Song: [Stationary Rose](https://open.spotify.com/track/2K3vzgzIVq3nn3uefGKdJQ?si=718cf0a592664426)

You dream of the [[Cosmic Court]]. The ritual sword is very interesting to most of them, and they take turns examining it, providing their thoughts.

“Curious,” says Dew of Ageless Grass, peering at the sword through his loupe, “this is most extraordinary. The blade is quite dull, like worn stone; its interior iridesces like a geode, and this is exposed through sharpening.”

“I see no reason to doubt the story put forth by that woman Kit,” offers Cat’s Eye of the Hurricane. “Iris of the Moon inspires honesty in everyone.” She holds her hand out toward the sword, gauging its energy. “Reanimation of a dead body is certainly odd, but the magic is strong enough.”

Four Clouds, Great Omen leaps from their seat and exclaims, “But what sort of magic even is it? No one in the Court recognizes it. It cannot be cosmic in nature. It certainly comes from some great sorcerer, like Lord Cetus (note to Alex: this is the whale’s title in the Court), but it… must be terrestrial.”

It is silent for a moment before Sparrows in the Steel Sky speaks—softly, as always. “Then the planet must have an Elder of its own.”

You thank them for their time.

## [[Reverb]]

Song: [K-03 Infernal Jazz](https://open.spotify.com/track/1G4FKtedXJ26C5hH9irsC4?si=83673a93447949d5)

You dream that you are the Dullahan. You are in a dark alleyway on a floor high up in the Spire. A mangled human lays at your feet. Their flesh is under your fingernails, but it does not bother you.

You reflect on your onslaught over the past two weeks. It has been most fruitful. On average, you killed 1.6 humans per night. You do not know why you have felt driven to hunt, but it does not bother you.

You collapse against the alley wall, your body finally giving out. [[Adeline]] slowly walks towards you, repeatedly tossing her sai into the air. You feel like you knew her once. “Astrid,” she sobs, “sorry to keep you waiting.” She goes in for the kill, but it does not bother you.
