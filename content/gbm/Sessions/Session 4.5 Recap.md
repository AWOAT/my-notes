
## [[Claire Grimoire]]

You once again dream of a dark forest. 

With knife in hand, you turn to face the fallen magical girl that has been chasing you. It is a grotesque version of [[Lady Aricia]]; it looks as though her head, arm, and leg have all swelled up and burst. You are no longer scared. You are capable. You have already killed her once, and this time will be no different. You stab its exposed brain—

Song: [K-03 Seraphim Jazz](https://open.spotify.com/track/2GHRsdc1SVv5O5q609C8Mb?si=6aecc8ae62a24e5d)

There is a flash, and suddenly you are [[Thistle from Five Thickets|Thistle]], back in the museum. Everything looks… fuzzy, muted. The fight has not been going well. Your glaive is close to splintering down the middle, and you are crumpled up on the floor, bleeding profusely from your stomach. [[Mistress of the Dark Flame Blade|Missy]] is on her way but will be too late. Across the room, Rail dives at Lady Aricia, but she swats him out of the air. The Beast lunges directly at Rail, jaws gaping, going for the kill. And though you have little strength, there is always more to give.

“...swap.”

Now it is you in front of the Beast, helpless, ready to die. It goes to rip out your heart, and as its teeth touch your chest, you wake up. 

**Harvested Ability:** You may instantaneously switch places with anyone, as long as you are truly willing to die for them. You must spend one link to do so; if this is another player character, the spent link must be with them.

## [[Iris of the Moon]]

Song: [Automobile Interior](https://open.spotify.com/track/1r6IWuUr70enYnLhepSHWT?si=4631ad7d6e754472)

You once again dream of the [[Cosmic Court]]. 

Cat’s Eye of the Hurricane clears her throat. “We have a lot to get through today, so let us begin. Firstly, Iris of the Moon, I have some information you might find useful. [[Lady Aricia|Kana Heartbreak]] was a member of the highest tier of the Harpoon. We have determined that they refer to themselves as the Anointed. Moreover, Crescent of Clover has essentially confirmed that at least two more of the Anointed are magical girls. There will be many people at Kleos’s upcoming fight, and they might be there. Please be careful. Next, we have a new member joining us. Sparrows in the Steel Sky, go ahead.”

Sparrows in the Steel Sky stands up from their chair and exits the room. After a few moments, they return with the newcomer. Your heart skips a beat as she walks in. This is true for most members of the Cosmic Court. After all, she is beautiful. She is wearing a long dress, made from the rarest flowers and stitched together by a fairy’s hand. The thorny crown on her head complements her perfectly serene expression. But your heart skips a beat for a different reason.

“Creatures of the court,” Sparrows in the Steel Sky announces, “I would like to present our newest member, [[Thistle from Five Thickets]].” Thistle curtsies, and you wake up.

The [[Bulwark]] is looking pretty hard for her so I figured she’s on your side. If you’re interested, I can put you in touch with my source.”

## [[Reverb]]
Song: [Alarming Incursion](https://open.spotify.com/track/5sGcs4ji3MsDiQDYnvJdnP?si=bf2ca8aeb11240f6)

You dream of a dining room. You, Adaigo, are at the head of a long, long table with a bountiful feast. Invisible servants bring you dishes to sample, and the fireplace is roaring cozily. And yet you shiver; from the other end of the table, Reverb shoots you an icy glare.

“This is not what I agreed to,” she says, “so let us get this straight. Our relationship is not as simple as you holding the reins until it is convenient. This body is equally ours, as decreed by the Whale. You’re that worried about [[Adeline]]? you’re that concerned with putting [[Claire Grimoire|Claire]] in her place? Remember what you have offered up as sacrifice: your identity, that which makes you… you.”

Emboldened, she continues. “You haven’t even asked me anything about that last dream we had. That’s not just my history. It’s ours. In accordance with your contract, the line between us will continue to blur until it ceases to exist at all. I suggest we start accepting it before it catches us by surprise. Am I making myself clear?”

You wake up before sunrise, unable to fall back asleep.
