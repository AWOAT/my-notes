
## [[Claire Grimoire]]

You dream of a dark forest. Your lantern sputters out as you wade through the river, but you must continue onward. There is an awful, horrible sound coming from behind you, and you must not look back at her. You stumble through the darkness for minutes, maybe hours, until the faint light of the bunker finally appears. You run up to the door. It is locked. You bang on it, shouting until your throat is raw, but it remains shut. Nobody is home. 

The corrupted magical girl continues to approach, and your time is running out. In your desperation, you call out to the Whale, and suddenly something materializes in your hand. Is it a key? No. It is a steak knife. Behind you, her tortured screams grow louder, and there is a sickening squelch whenever she takes a step. She is certainly no longer human. You grit your teeth and turn to fight—and you wake up in a cold sweat.

It feels warm now, much warmer than the blizzard did…

## [[Reverb]]

Song: [Rust and Moss](https://open.spotify.com/track/3jAH0zzTKlNzVTJl9tN12p?si=96c4865d9f54474f)

You dream of a coral reef. You sit in an open area where a sunbeam warms the sand, and you sing. The toadfish in the coral sing back to you. You are not used to singing underwater, but the toadfish are not used to listening at all, so they don’t mind too much. Together you pass the time, basking in the sun. 

Eventually night falls. The toadfish return to their dens but insist that you continue singing; after all, you’ve gotten a lot better throughout the day. You try to continue, but your voice is so frail when you are alone. Then the Whale’s deep, primordial voice begins to resonate from the coral itself. You pick up the melody and sing with it until morning. When the toadfish wake up, they thank you, and you slowly wake.

You are on patrol in [[Infestation]] when you see [[Mistress of the Dark Flame Blade]] slaying an alien. She is wielding her pristine blade, which radiates pitch-black flames from its scratchless surface. At the sight of it, Reverb tenses in confusion. She senses something that she should have sensed long ago: the sword originally belonged to an alien. You may ask any one question as a follow-up.

Why is she using an alien sword specifically? Asking intentions and origins of the weapon.
