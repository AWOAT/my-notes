## The Fight

The group went to sleep and arrived at the Golden Colosseum. They looked around at the many new magical girls and killed some time before the fight started.

[[Iris of the Moon|Iris]] was following a lead on [[Naroma, the South Star|Naroma]], [[Hera]]’s new mentee. [[Reverb]] and Hera had a sassy conversation while the others chatted with Naroma. They’re a new magical girl, with some information on Dr. Erica Pitkin. She was hiding with [[Breaking Ground]] for a bit, but she started volunteering at Kitchenette. Naroma also said that they had access to some clearance levels at WML, but not all of them. This might get more difficult now that they’re a magical girl, since they will soon be installing superpower detectors.

Everybody sat down as the fight started. Our protagonists sat next to [[Gauze]]. A gong played, and Kleos appeared and gave a speech.

“Thank you for coming to this duel between myself, Kleos, and the challenger, [[Adeline]]. Taking part in this duel establishes both participants as creatures of virtue. The loser’s name will be etched into the cosmic wheel, and the universe will recognize their undying glory. The winner retains, or may take for their own, the position of Kleos."

Gauze was astonished at this. Kleos’s voice, the glass beads it wielded… this was [[Nylla]]. Gauze had never seen this before, and she was now quite scared to leave this timeline.

As Adeline entered the combat area, [[Claire Grimoire|Claire]] got in front of her and said:
“Oh, I just wanted to come down. I hope you do well! Please come back okay! …think that’s Astrid would have said? Do you? No… I don’t think it is. Because you murdered her. I think she would be laughing while you died, just like I will be. Break a leg.”

This broke Adeline’s spirit, giving her -1d for the whole combat.

The fight was quite brutal. Adeline attacked with a massive sword but was easily countered. When she summoned multiple sais, Kleos created strands of glass beads and yoinked them back at Adeline’s body. Kleos went in for the kill; Adeline blocked it for a moment, but Kleos turned Adeline’s whole lower body into glass. She lost her strength and was stabbed gloriously through the chest. Her body remained on the ground until it faded into stardust.


## The Reception

After the fight, the crew went to the reception area, fitted with drinks and snacks. Although it was a dream, it still tasted good. Claire and Iris went around meeting new people.

They met [[Ipsen the Great]]. She was a happy young girl with way too much goddamn energy. She was playing hacky-sack and her feet moved so fast that they generated electricity. She insisted on “the Great” being said as part of her name, but she quickly warmed up to Claire and Iris and told them they could just call her Ipsen. She was the culprit who drank all of the reception’s coffee. She had a bit of beef with [[Techo]] but only because Techo didn’t want to talk that much. Everybody loves Ipsen!!! 

Iris wants to make an app that lets magical girls communicate more easily. The Everything App.

They met [[Tourmaline]] and [[Kyanite]], two members of the [[Knighthawks]]. They learn that each member has some control over an element: Tourmaline over fire, Kyanite over water (see descriptions on the Colosseum sheet). Also, apparently the Knighthawks aren’t all originally knights. The two leaders are, as well as the founder.

For example, Kyanite’s transcended self was made completely of water. Once she changed her contract and signed up with the Knighthawks, her true self changed.


Claire and Iris pressed them a little to learn about the founder, but they’re under strict orders. They pushed a little too hard, and the leader [[Euclase]] surprised them from behind. She was very intimidating, shook both their hands and asserted her dominance. 

Euclase confirms for Iris that the knights don’t really have a code. “Our code is just to kill big shit.” She also calls Iris a knight-lite, which really made her angry. But they were already intimidated; no place to talk back. 


[[Lovelace]] approaches the duo. They wanted to talk with Reverb because Lovelace is also a Harmony; they are the superposition of the bodies of Rosalind and Amie. They’re quite chill: Iris gathered info. Lovelace could probably win in a 1v1 against any PC (after all, it’s two people). Rosalind has a penchant for violence, but there are no ill intentions in their search for Reverb.

Their bodies inhabit the same space, so it looks like both of them at the same time. You can focus on one if you try really hard. They don’t share thoughts, but their voices speak at the same time. A little confusing but not relevant for much, just neat. 

Claire investigated the murder of Vance Thomas by asking [[Naroma, the South Star|Naroma]] a few more questions. Naroma would kill to keep a certain secret: the identity of the murderer. It wasn’t Hera or Naroma, but it was someone who was present at the Colosseum.

Claire and [[Hebrides]] make eye contact. They are both incredibly dangerous and scary. No wonder Hebrides doesn’t have any partners.


Iris did not get much from the [[Knighthawks]] in relation to her contract with the whale, so she goes to talk with [[Jet]] and [[Evelyn Revoir|Evelyn]]. They both trained at the Whale’s [[Cosmic Dojo]], where the Whale was their sensei. The dojo had many rooms from which they could hear other people training, but they were never visible. 
One of their guest teachers was Crescent of Clover, a known member of Iris’s Cosmic Court. 

The martial artists have a code: exclusively self-defense (only attacking aliens that pose a threat), and no asking for outside help.

After talking with everyone they wanted to, Iris and Claire left.


Meanwhile, Reverb talked to a few people on their own, and someone two days later.

She talked with Jet. Jet is quite hesitant to talk about her past, and she thought Adeline was too prideful. Also, at full power, Jet gives herself a 35% chance to beat Kleos. But accepting the duel almost certainly goes against the self-defense code, so she could never be at full power.

She talked with [[Palette]]. Definitely the most coolheaded member of [[Beyond the Veil|BTV]]. Palette speaks concisely and with intent. Viewed from any angle, Palette appears flat. Her movements are a series of oil paintings (just like Loving Vincent). 

She carries around spray cans and paint buckets, because she doesn’t actually have control over paint. Instead, she can merge into any painted surface, move through it, and emerge elsewhere (just like Splatoon).

BTV is probably going to claim part of the floor Autumn Rain (similar in danger to Infestation or Cropnik).

Later, she goes on a chill patrol with [[Lovelace]] in Autumn Rain. Their two selves were coworkers before they merged. Lovelace was just looking to chat; they don’t have a crew, and they got into a scuffle with the [[The House|House]] when they showed up in [[Cropnik]].
