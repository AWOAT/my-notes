## Mission 

Everybody gets a notification. There has been some sort of disaster in a museum on a lower floor, and multiple people have died. Some of them are only presumed dead, and one of these is [[Thistle from Five Thickets|Thistle]]. After a bit of deliberation, the group hurries down.

They met [[Techo|Tiffany Mahlon]], author of that article on Vance Thomas. She was vaguely magical. There were some other reporters there too, but the cops were preventing people from entering. The crew ended up sneaking in.

Note for Mercedes: Vaguely magical, but not a magical girl? No. That’s not right. It’s very faint, but it’s there. You know her, intimately. Even though Tiffany is shielding it, hiding her presence… you feel like you’re back on that rooftop, sharing her dinner. This is [[Techo]]. You meet each other’s gaze. She knows that you know, and she has never, ever been more petrified.

The cops followed them in. They didn’t pose much of a threat, but they made noise as they chased, alerting the enemy to their presence.

Some of the building had collapsed; it looked like collateral damage from a magical girl fight. There were mauled corpses as well. The crew followed the noise made by Rail and opened a door.

Inside, [[Mistress of the Dark Flame Blade]] was fighting against [[Lady Aricia]] (Kana Heartbreak) and the Beast. To the side was Thistle’s corpse, with her skull split open. 

Claire started off by singling out the Beast, but it dodged her uppercut and attacked from behind. Iris was there to absorb the hit, though. From there, the fight went much better, and Claire ended up punting the puppy through the glass covering of an exhibit. 

Poisoned Promise: [[Hera]] started mentoring a magical girl named [[Naroma, the South Star]].
For Adaigo: same girl from Session 3.5 rumor

At the same time, Missy was not doing so well, so Reverb and Iris fought Lady Aricia. Reverb shouted to launch Aricia into the wall, but was slightly hurt by the shadow clone she sent forth. Iris Tom and Jerry’d her with a massive shield bash. Aricia turned off the lights, but the crew could still sense her position. Reverb attempted to resonate with her body but failed. Iris shared the pain and pinned Aricia down.

With the fight over, Claire ate a blighted heart from Thistle’s head. Missy and Aricia both said that it was there because of a spell that Aricia cast. Aricia’s goal is to revive the whale by reducing the number of blighted hearts. This happens when either aliens or magical girls are killed, but this spell is needed to make them harvestable from magical girls.

Claire kills Aricia with a spike to the brain. Missy offers to use the spell on Aricia, but the crew is very against it. Iris and Claire carry out Thistle’s body as Reverb talks with Missy. It was a very, very good conversation. Highlighted Reverb’s resolve, and how fighting the aliens did not require them to desecrate the bodies of fallen magical girls. Missy agreed, and she destroyed Aricia’s body so she could not be harvested.

Claire and Iris met with a devastated [[Techo]]. She and Claire acknowledged each other, and she had a long hug with Iris. She carried off Thistle’s body as Rail accompanied her.
