## Death

Died to Kleos after a demoralizing insult from [[Claire Grimoire|Claire]], though she probably would have lost anyways.


## Season 1 Actions 


### Session 2.5

Slayed the Dullahan, the corrupted body of her old partner Astrid.

Talked with [[Reverb]] for the first time. Went on a hunt together, to kill some time.

### Session 3

Went hunting with [[Reverb]]. Found a Schistatylus, and realized it would drop a Kleos invitation. Adeline wants to get the PCs to help fight it. They do, and she swoops in and accepts the invitation.

### Session 4

Tells [[Reverb]] that she'll let her know about the Kleos fight.

### Session 5

Was taunted by [[Claire Grimoire|Claire]] about having to kill Astrid.

Demoralized, she fought Kleos and died brutally.
