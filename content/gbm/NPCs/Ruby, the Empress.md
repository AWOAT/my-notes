## Description

Ruby used to be exquisitely well-dressed, with a long black dress, fur coat, pearl necklace and earrings. Recently, she has fully sacrificed her humanity and Eclipsed. Since then, she has appeared much more manic and animal-like.

Rejects traditional weapons, strikes with pure wealth. Could buy the weapons, for sure. Could buy anything. Red carpet appears wherever she walks.

Able to turn things or people into gold. Frequently uses molten gold to make a statement.



## Magical Life

Official leader of the [[Orcas]]. Very headstrong; trusted [[Techo]]'s opinions but felt a little undermined. Saw [[Thistle from Five Thickets|Thistle]] as too soft.

Hates the PCs, but especially [[Claire Grimoire|Claire]]. Does anything she can to screw her over, and she has no qualms about other magical girls getting hurt in the crossfire.




## Mundane Life

Very destitute, has scraped by through selling alien data to anyone willing to buy: [[Codex Corps]], [[WML]], [[Harpoon]]. 

Screwed over by the system. Was once selected to be Bait: managed to escape, but had no identity to use for the rest of her life.
