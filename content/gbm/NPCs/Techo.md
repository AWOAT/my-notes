## Description

Techo is a detective, judge, jury, and executioner. She wears a long trench coat, almost like a wedding train. She carries a binder of facts, figures, and strategies, as well as a pen and notepad. She often dons a Venetian mask to maintain the anonymity of the jury.

She can sense whenever someone is lying to her. 

Techo can summon gavels, scales, and guillotines. Aliens usually don't have many qualities to redeem themselves with.



Techo views trustworthiness as the highest of all virtues. 



## Magical Life

Techo was a member of the [[Orcas]], trusted implicitly by them, although she understood that [[Ruby, the Empress|Ruby]] was often too heavy-handed. Techo's say had the most sway.

She cared deeply for [[content/gbm/NPCs/Thistle from Five Thickets|Thistle]] and was quite devastated by her death. She was also quite fond of [[Iris of the Moon|Iris]], who was the first to discover her mundane identity.

The magical girl [[Naroma, the South Star|Naroma]] is her sister.


## Mundane Life

Tiffany Mahlon was a journalist in the [[Great Slice]], but due to the corrupt nature of the Spire, she was forced to cover up many stories. When she received her powers, she realized it was her chance to take matters into her own hands. 

Her sister is [[Naroma, the South Star|Lilliana Mahlon]]. 


## Death 

After killing the corrupt civilians Vance Thomas and Mayor Heffernan, Techo received the revelation that she herself was complicit in the abuse of the Spire. Following her [[Fire Seal]], the sacrifice of her morals was complete, and in despair, she ended her own life as atonement.
