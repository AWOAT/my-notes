Biography: 
From a young age, Mercedes really took to Austen and Shakespear and English literature in general, despite her poor upbringing. She is incredibly close with her mother and three younger siblings (her dad is quite detached, victim of an alien attack). But she lives alone now and works as a firefighter. She strives to embody her ideals, but it is very difficult in a profession facing constant tragedy.
One night, the Whale appeared to her in a dream. Mercedes was floating in space, completely alone. He invited her to join the Cosmic Court, and she took a vow to become one of its knights, swearing off her family. The greatest harm you can do to your family is make them grieve.
When she transforms, she embodies her true self, Iris of the Moon. As a protector, she specializes in shield conjuration, all emblazoned with the reflection of the moon in the Whale’s eye. She wears a dark-black, lacey, loose fitting dress, just like a lady of the court.
