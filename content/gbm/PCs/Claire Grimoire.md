Biography:
Elinalaise is from the protected, wealthiest area, home of the oligarchs. Her father is a member of the Luminous Council (essentially the Illuminati), and as a result, her life is nearly completely scheduled. She is a member of a stifling high society, with nearly no will of her own.
One night, the Whale appeared to her in a dream. Elinalaise was chained and locked in a prison cell. Oh, what she would do for power, for freedom… Perhaps she would even have made a deal with the Devil himself, but a contract with the Whale certainly sufficed.
Everything changes when she transforms into Claire. Now, she is a magical girl who wields blood-based weapons and hunts the aliens on the upper floors whenever she can sneak away from her mundane life. Her outfit transforms from a Victorian era dress to something more modern, a black and red miniskirt.
Aliens are just something to kill. Nothing is scarier than her usual lack of autonomy. 
Everybody in this world is only looking out for themselves.
