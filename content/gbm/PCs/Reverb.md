Biography:
Adagio was a former piece of Bait for the Lure. She had escaped from them often and lived on the streets of Gutterwal. Homeless for most of her life, but now has a stable job in Block 28 as a street sweeper.
One night, the Whale appeared to her in a dream. It called to her with a deep melodic voice and told her stories of its past and her future. It helped her avoid capture and live a better life. It gave her a more personal guide: Reverb, a spirit who lives inside her and shares her body. 
When she transforms, the two halves merge into one. Reverb appears as a conductor and uses sound waves and a conductor’s baton to destroy her foes. It took a while for this unity to be harmonious and not stifling, but now they can work together, for the most part.
