## Description

Thistle is very earthy. She has vibrant hair, arranged in a single braid with ivy woven in. She grows petal wings, which she can use to glide or flap once with, after an insect glaive launch. She wears a flower crown made from extinct species. Thorny clothes. 

Could switch places with Rail, her woodpecker, as both of them were willing to die for each other. She uses the Insect Glaive.


Thistle is very environmentally focused, and overall easygoing. She does not want enemies.

## Rail of Yearly Journey

Rail is a woodpecker. He has a white body, black secondary, and red highlights. Could switch places with Thistle, as both of them were willing to die for each other.

Assisted the PCs in Session 6.



## Magical Life

Found Rail being experimented on by [[Breaking Ground]], and broke him out.

Thistle was part of the [[Orcas]]. She was the complete opposite of [[Ruby, the Empress|Ruby]], but Thistle never really held Ruby's anger against her. 

Was always a friend to the PCs, even going out of her way to apologize for Ruby's actions.


## Mundane Life

Heather Mysie was a museum employee, serving as a tour guide and curator-in-training. Her father was the corrupt director of the museum, and she was very complicit in his crimes.


## Death

Killed by [[Lady Aricia]] in her museum.

Harvested by [[Claire Grimoire|Claire]].

Thistle now appears frequently in the [[Cosmic Court]], providing aid and insight to [[Iris of the Moon|Iris]]. She now wears a long dress, the rarest flowers stitched together by a fairy's hand.
